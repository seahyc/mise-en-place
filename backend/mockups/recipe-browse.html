Pad Thai with Prawns
🇹🇭 Thai 25 min · Serves 2 📝 3 edits
▶
Imported from @cookingwithmaria on TikTok
Classic Thai stir-fried noodles with juicy prawns, tangy tamarind, and crunchy bean sprouts. A weeknight favourite that's ready in 25 minutes.
Ingredients
🍜200grice noodles
🦐150gprawns, peeled
🥚2eggs
🐟3 tbspfish sauce
🫙1 tbsptamarind paste
🌱100gbean sprouts
🧅2spring onions, sliced
🥜2 tbspcrushed peanuts
🍋1lime, cut into wedges
Steps
1
Soak rice noodles in warm water for 30 minutes until soft, then drain thoroughly
2
Heat wok over high heat, add oil, scramble eggs until just set, push to the side
3
Add prawns, cook 2 minutes until pink. Add noodles, fish sauce, tamarind — toss for 2 minutes
4
Add bean sprouts and spring onions, toss 30 seconds. Serve with crushed peanuts and lime
