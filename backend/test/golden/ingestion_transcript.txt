Today I'm going to show you how to make the perfect pasta carbonara.
First, you'll need spaghetti, guanciale, eggs, pecorino romano, and black pepper.
Start by boiling a large pot of salted water for the pasta.
While the water heats up, cut the guanciale into small strips.
Cook the guanciale in a cold pan on medium heat until crispy.
In a bowl, mix the egg yolks with grated pecorino and black pepper.
Cook the spaghetti until al dente, then reserve some pasta water.
Toss the hot pasta with the guanciale off the heat.
Add the egg mixture and toss quickly, adding pasta water as needed.
Serve immediately with extra pecorino and black pepper on top.
